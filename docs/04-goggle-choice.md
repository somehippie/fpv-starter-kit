# 4. Analog Goggle Choice — Skyzone SKY04X Pro

**Purchased: Skyzone SKY04X Pro** — $533 with the **SKF** discount code and free shipping ($538 → $533 effective total).

## Options evaluated

| Goggles | Verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Skyzone SKY04X Pro** | ✅ Chosen | Native analog support, DVR, good stock availability, strong community support, range upgradeable via antenna swaps |
| **HDZero Goggle 2** | Close second | Strong digital image quality, but analog is only a secondary/adapter feature rather than a native mode; also had cost and availability concerns |
| **DJI Goggles 2** | Rejected | Digital-only (O3/O4) — fully incompatible with an analog video system, ruling it out outright for this build |

## Why SKY04X Pro over HDZero Goggle 2

Both were credible options, but the deciding factors were: native (not bolted-on) analog support matching the analog VTX choice (see [Analog vs. Digital](03-analog-vs-digital.md)), better in-stock availability at time of purchase, a stronger support/community base for troubleshooting, and a clear upgrade path on range via swappable antennas rather than being capped by the stock hardware.

## Timing note

Watched for Black Friday timing vs. buying now — sell-out and price-increase risk on analog-friendly goggles like the SKY04X Pro, plus general 2025–2027 US-China tariff pressure pushing FPV retailer prices upward over time, tipped the decision toward buying now rather than waiting. This was reinforced by seeing the Meteor75 Pro (drone side) already sell out during the same shopping window (see [Drone, Battery & Spares](05-drone-battery-spares.md)).
